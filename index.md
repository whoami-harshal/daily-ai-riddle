# Riddle Streak

**A new AI mystery every day.** Read a strange story, ask the AI host yes/no questions, uncover the key clues and explain what really happened. Keep your 🔥 streak alive and share a spoiler-free result card. Mysteries in English, Bahasa Indonesia and Hindi / Hinglish. Riddle Streak is an app on [Anna](https://anna.partners).
