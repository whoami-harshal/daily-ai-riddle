# Riddle Streak — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Riddle Streak is an app that runs inside Anna. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What is sent to Anna's AI

To make and check riddles and to judge your answers, the app sends these to Anna's built-in AI service (`anna.llm.complete`):

- your chosen riddle language and difficulty,
- a list of your recent riddle answers (so the AI doesn't repeat them),
- the riddle and the guesses you type, when they need to be judged.

The AI is provided by Anna and runs under your own Anna account and AI quota. Riddle Streak has no servers of its own and does not send your information anywhere else.

## What is stored

The app stores only what it needs to work, in **your own Anna app storage** (it is not shared with other users), plus a backup copy in this device's browser storage so your game can resume even offline or after the app window closes:

- your settings (riddle language, difficulty, theme),
- today's riddle and your guesses and hints for it,
- your current practice riddle,
- the answers of your last 30 riddles (to avoid repeats),
- your stats: streak, best streak, riddles solved and hints used.

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, location or any other personal data. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to. The **Copy / Share** buttons copy a result card or riddle text to your clipboard (or open your phone's share sheet). Result cards never include the answer.

## Deleting your data

Open **⚙️ Stats & settings → Reset all data** to erase everything the app has stored. Uninstalling the app from Anna also removes access to it.

## Children

Riddles are written and checked to be family-friendly. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
